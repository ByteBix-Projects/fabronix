About Fabronix Industries LLP

Established on 29-05-2026 in Rajkot, Gujarat, Fabronix Industries LLP operates as a registered Limited Liability Partnership under ROC Ahmedabad. Our operations are structurally defined under the National Industrial Classification (NIC) code 25999: Manufacture of other fabricated metal products n.e.c. (not elsewhere classified).

Rajkot is renowned as India's prominent industrial cluster for mechanical engineering, metallurgy, and fabrication. Operating from this prime industrial ecosystem, Fabronix leverages advanced infrastructure, skilled labor pools, and direct material supply lines to provide cost-effective, premium metal fabrication services.
